wahoo_receiver.stl is cut from Wahoo Fitness's ELEMNT BOLT 2 mount base
(Mount_base-bolt2 in https://github.com/WahooFitness/ELEMNT-Mount, BOLT2_MOUNT/),
which Wahoo publishes under the MIT License below. extract_receiver.py made it.

Wahoo's note: the receiver was designed for injection-moulded Nylon 6 with 10% glass
fill; other materials change spring force, retention and removal torque. Use at your
own risk.
